**Subject:** Permission request: short excerpts of Sheikh Mishary Alafasy's recitation in non-commercial Qur'an education videos

Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh,

My name is Abdusattar Tursunkul. I run Yassir, an online Qur'an school in Kyrgyzstan where students
memorise the Qur'an and learn its meaning with a teacher (ustaz). In October 2026 we started a small
Instagram page, **@quran.yassir**, for Russian-speaking Muslims in Central Asia.

Our videos are short (about 30 seconds) and educational: one ayah is recited, each word lights up on the
Mushaf page as it is recited, and then we show a verified Russian meaning translation (E. Kuliev) and a short
line from a classical tafsir (as-Sa'di, Ibn Kathir). The aim is to help people understand the words they
already recite in their prayers. There is no music, nothing is laid over the recitation, there are no
advertisements and the page is not monetised.

The recitation we have used is Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy's — no voice is closer to our viewers' hearts.
We have published three videos, each using one short excerpt:

| Video | Ayah | Length of excerpt | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| "One word: هُدًى" | 1:6 (twice) and 2:2 (words 6–7) | about 15 s in total | https://www.instagram.com/p/DeIxNsghKph/ |
| "Is it noisy inside you too?" | 13:28 (words 7–11, twice) | about 14 s in total | https://www.instagram.com/p/DeI5-o7MYzI/ |
| "Du'a for our children" | 25:74 | about 17 s | https://www.instagram.com/p/DeJ-gJKMSJv/ |

We would like to ask for your permission to continue using **short excerpts (up to about 20 seconds per
video)** of Sheikh Mishary's recitation in this way — in non-commercial, educational videos only, never with
music, always with the recitation unaltered. We will gladly credit the recitation in every caption in the
form you prefer (for example: "Recitation: Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy"), and link to your app if you wish.

If you would rather we did not use the recitation, please tell us and we will remove the excerpts from the
published videos and stop using them.

Jazakum Allahu khairan for the recitation that has brought so many people closer to the Qur'an, and for
your time.

Abdusattar Tursunkul
